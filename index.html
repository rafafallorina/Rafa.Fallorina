02 — THE ARCHIVE

MORE WORK.

My work often begins with hands-on creativity. I manually illustrate design assets such as logos, typography, and visual elements before refining and elevating them digitally. Beyond graphic design, I have also explored product photography and 2D motion graphics, combining multiple visual assets with hand-drawn elements to create more dynamic and engaging compositions.
